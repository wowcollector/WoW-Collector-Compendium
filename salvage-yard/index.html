Salvage Yard Appearance Sources

The Level 3 https://www.wowhead.com/building=141/salvage-yard building in your Garrison can be a treasure trove of appearance sources that, other than Quantum tokens, cannot be acquired in any other way. The appearances themselves can be acquired by making a class trial for each class, if you're not interested in collecting every source.

These appearance sources have a chance to be acquired when salvaging https://www.wowhead.com/item=140590/large-crate-of-salvage#comments, which are potential bonus rewards from Garrison missions. These:

  • Only drop while the Salvage Yard is built and level 3.
  • Are not guaranteed rewards from missions, other than level 100 missions.
  • Only have a chance to be rewarded from level 95-100 missions.

Furthermore, they are not guaranteed to contain a cosmetic appearance. More commonly, they will contain Garrison Resources, various Warlords crafting reagents or follower upgrade tokens.

IMPORTANT! Do not spam-salvage these crates! Wait 2-4 seconds between each one! Salvaging them too quickly one after the other will eat your loot! Why? Spaghetti code.

Consult your ATT list to see all available appearance sources (there's lots of them).

Unlocking the Salvage Yard

Yes, if you wish to collect all these appearances, you'll need a Salvage Yard on all 11 classes that were in-game when Warlords launched. Yes, the Salvage Yard is the only level 1 building that you need to do a quest-line to unlock. Of course it is.

To get the level 1 blueprints, https://www.wowhead.com/item=111957/salvage-yard-level-1, you need be at least level 30 and somehow make your way to Spires of Arak on Draenor. Then:

  • Travel to Pinchwhistle Gearworks and pick up the following 3 quests:
    • https://www.wowhead.com/quest=35077/defungination 
    • https://www.wowhead.com/quest=35079/spore-be-gone 
    • https://www.wowhead.com/quest=36179/unwanted-pests 
  • Complete the quests, and continue the chain until you get https://www.wowhead.com/quest=35298/flame-on 
  • Complete Flame on, congratulations, you have the blueprint. It took you 15 minutes. For 11 classes, you'll have spent 3 hours doing this.
    • Bonus: You also unlock https://www.wowhead.com/quest=36062/kimzee-pinchwhistle#comments.
  • Go to the Garrison, start building the Salvage Yard level 1.
  • Buy the https://www.wowhead.com/item=111976/salvage-yard-level-2 blueprint from your Garrison Blueprints vendor, and upgrade your building when possible.
  • Complete this achievement https://www.wowhead.com/achievement=9468/salvaging-pays-off to unlock the blueprint for the level 3 Salvage Yard. You're finally done.
    • You'll have to purchase the blueprint on subsequent characters.
