Classic Instances

Usually, the best way to farm an instance these days is to send either your Druid or your Subtlety Rogue to zoom through at breakneck pace. However, if you're looking to collect everything, you won't be able to do it on a single character all the time!

This page aims to show the absolute minimum amount of characters required to 100% an instance. It is assumed the goal is to collect everything that actually gives you something, so this includes appearance sources, even if you own the actual appearance. Furthermore, if something in these instances has an alternative acquisition source that is easier, it is assumed to be the preferred way to acquire said item and will likely not be mentioned here.

Raids

Molten Core

  • Requires a minimum of 2-3 characters to fully complete.

There are four distinct loot restrictions in place:

  • Bind-on-Pickup recipes for Leatherworking, Blacksmithing and Engineering.
    • Reduce minimum requirement to 2 characters if a character has 2 of those recipes.
  • https://www.wowhead.com/item=138833/illusion-flametongue can only be learned by a Shaman.
  • https://www.wowhead.com/item=19019/thunderfury-blessed-blade-of-the-windseeker can only be equipped by 9 classes.
  • https://www.wowhead.com/item=17182/sulfuras-hand-of-ragnaros can only be equipped by 6 classes.

Naturally, for the low-drop nature of the items required to create the legendary weapons, running multiple eligible characters through Molten Core is advised.

Ideal Composition

  • A Shaman with Leatherworking.
    • Will also be eligible for the Illusion and the Sulfuras legendary.
  • A Warrior, Paladin or Death Knight with Blacksmithing and Engineering.
    • Will also be eligible for the Sulfuras and Thunderfury legendaries.

Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj

  • Requires a minimum of 9 characters to fully complete.

There are 3 distinct loot restrictions in place:

  • Each of the original Classic classes has 2 quests which award a Cloak and a Weapon. To complete these quests, quest items from the bosses in Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj are required.
    • To complete the Weapon quest, characters must be Exalted with the Cenarion Circle.
  • A vendor sells 2 Blacksmithing Plans after the General Rajaxx encounter (see ATT tooltip for information on how to trigger him).
    • These recipes can be bought at Revered and Exalted with Cenarion Circle
  • Seven Bind-on-Pickup Enchanting recipes have a small chance to drop from any boss in this raid as well as the Temple of Ahn'Qiraj. They are more quickly farmed here than in the Temple.
    • 2 of these recipes are also sold by certain reputation vendors in Outland, meaning only 5 of them are exclusive common boss drops to these two instances.

Ideal Composition

  • One of each of the original classes: Mage, Priest, Warlock, Druid, Rogue, Hunter, Shaman, Paladin Warrior.
    • One of these classes should be a Blacksmith.
    • At least one of these classes should be an Enchanter.

Temple of Ahn'Qiraj

  • Requires a minimum of 9 characters to fully complete.

There are 3 distinct loot restrictions in place:

  • Each of the original Classic classes has 5 quests which award tier piece armor. To complete these quests:
    • Items that drop from bosses in the Temple are required.
    • Up to Honored reputation with Brood of Nozdormu is required.
  • The same Enchanting recipes in the Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj drop here as well, in the same manner.
  • A class able to deal Frost damage and have quick attack speed to defeat Viscidus.

There are also 2 quests to acquire Weapon appearances. These may be completed on any class and will award all other appearances from the quest.

Ideal Composition

  • One of each of the original classes: Mage, Priest, Warlock, Druid, Rogue, Hunter, Shaman, Paladin Warrior.
    • At least one of these classes should be an Enchanter.
  • A class that is able to deal Frost damage and have quick attack speed. Generally, this is one of the following:
    • A Rogue with a Weapon Enchant that deals Frost damage.
    • A Frost Death Knight with two one-handed weapons equipped, though this raises the minimum character composition by 1.
    • See the comments on the https://www.wowhead.com/npc=15299/viscidus page for a plethora of other strategies on how to tackle this boss, including using minions spawned from trinkets!

Dungeons

Blackrock Depths

  • Requires a minimum of 2-4 characters.

There are 4 distinct loot restrictions in place:

  • A BoP Jewelcrafting recipe that drops from Golem Lord Argelmach.
  • A BoP Enchanting recipe that drops from Lord Roccor.
  • Two BoP Blacksmithing recipes that spawn as interactable objects throughout the dungeon.
  • A BoP Alchemy recipe that is sold by Plugger Spazzring

Furthermore, the Thorium Brotherhood quartermaster resides here. Getting reputation with them is easy (see link) and can be done through many avenues not directly related to this dungeon, so we won't cover that here. For reference, the following professions have recipes sold by the quartermaster: Enchanting, Leatherworking, Tailoring, Blacksmithing, Alchemy.

Ideal Composition

  • A character with two of the required professions (for example Jewelcrafting and Blacksmithing)
  • A character with the other two required professions (Jewelcrafting and Enchanting)
  • Usually, players do not pick any these professions together, so generally you are looking at 4 characters to 100% Blackrock Depths.

Deadmines

  • Requires a minimum of 1 Rogue.

There are 1-2 distinct loot restrictions in place:

  • The https://www.wowhead.com/quest=44930/deadmines-seain-red quest and cosmetic ensemble reward can only be completed by an Outlaw Rogue that owns the https://www.wowhead.com/item=8492/parrot-cage-green-wing-macaw pet.

The https://www.wowhead.com/item=120138/tome-of-polymorph-monkey#comments has a chance to drop from Mining Monkey enemies, but only drops for Mages. This item can also drop from Hozen in various spots around Pandaria, and may be easier to acquire there (see WoWhead comments).

Dire Maul

  • Requires a minimum of 1-4 characters, one of which has to be a Warlock.

There are 4 distinct loot restrictions in place:

  • Pusilin drops https://www.wowhead.com/item=207297/grimoire-of-the-felblaze-imp, only for Warlocks.
  • Pusilin also drops https://www.wowhead.com/item=18267/recipe-runn-tum-tuber-surprise, a BoP Cooking recipe.
  • https://www.wowhead.com/npc=14338/knot-thimblejack teaches https://www.wowhead.com/spell=22813/gordok-ogre-suit for Tailors and https://www.wowhead.com/spell=22815/gordok-ogre-suit (again) for Leatherworkers.

Ideal Composition

  • A Warlock with Classic Tailoring and Classic Cooking.
  • Another character with Classic Leatherworking.

Gnomeregan

  • Requires an Engineer.

https://www.wowhead.com/item=11828/schematic-pet-bombling is BoP and drops from the last boss of the dungeon.

Lower Blackrock Spire

  • Requires a Jewelcrafter.

https://www.wowhead.com/item=21955/design-black-diamond-crab is BoP and drops from https://www.wowhead.com/npc=9736/quartermaster-zigris.

Scarlet Halls

  • Requires at least 2 characters - 1 Mail, 1 Plate.

https://www.wowhead.com/item=23192/tabard-of-the-scarlet-crusade (BoP) drops from https://www.wowhead.com/npc=58632/armsmaster-harlan and is required to access https://www.wowhead.com/npc=108785/scarlet-quartermaster during Darkmoon Faire, which sells 2 ensembles only learnable by Mail and Plate classes, respectively.

Old Scholomance

Requires 1 character with Classic Tailoring to learn https://www.wowhead.com/spell=26086/felcloth-bag#comments from a book that spawns on the floor after defeating https://www.wowhead.com/npc=10503/jandice-barov. 

Stratholme

  • Requires 1 Blacksmith to farm Banshees on the Service Entrance side for https://www.wowhead.com/item=74274/plans-phantom-blade#dropped-by;mode:none 

The Temple of Atal'Hakkar (Sunken Temple)

Requires 1 Leatherworker to farm the following Zone Drop BoP recipes:

  • https://www.wowhead.com/item=78346/pattern-green-dragonscale-breastplate
  • https://www.wowhead.com/item=78345/pattern-green-dragonscale-leggings

Zul'Farrak

Requires 1 character that can wield 1H (and possibly 2H) swords to equip https://www.wowhead.com/item=9379/sangthraze-the-deflector?bonus=6710 and https://www.wowhead.com/item=11086/jangthraze-the-protector?bonus=6710 in order to activate their Use effect and acquire https://www.wowhead.com/item=9372/sulthraze-the-lasher?bonus=6712.
