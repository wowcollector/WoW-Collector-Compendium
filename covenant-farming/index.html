Covenant Farming

Farming everything you can get from Covenants is a headache. First of all, there's a lot to get. Then, it's really only efficient to have a character focus on one Covenant, considering the investment required to upgrade buildings (which increase your anima gain from various forms of content). Then, there's even more - items that only drop for certain specs, ensembles that can only be learned by some classes, and then items that can be acquired from more than one covenant, but which one does it the easiest?

Where to even begin?

General Overview

Let's try and classify the different type of loot restrictions we have to deal with:

  • Loot dropping from various building content, such as the Anima Conductor or the Transport Network, can be armor type-restricted or spec-restricted.
  • Lots of stuff dropping from The Avowed content in Revendreth (with several kinds of restrictions) can more easily be farmed by a Venthyr character with a built up Ember Court.
  • Some Ensembles (like the Court of Night ones) require both the appropriate armor class and being a Night Fae Covenant member to learn.

In an ideal world, you'd have all 12 Shadowlands classes for each Covenant and just take it from there, but that's not only excessive but extremely boring to set up. Thus, to try and simplify this, this guide will break down everything that requires a specific class or spec or armor type. See the table below for a summary of where you should send who, and read further below to get an overview of all content that has those restrictions.

The following table is ordered by how much a class can farm from a Covenant. It does not take into account systems like the Abomination Factory of the Venthyr's Transport Network, which requires 4 characters to complete in full, though it does for example consider the 4 daggers from the Transport Network. It was created with the following considerations:

  • The 1st choice can farm the most loot-restricted content. It is the character to invest the most Anima on, for buildings and such.
  • The 2nd choice always has access to some loot-restricted content.
  • The 3rd and 4th choices either also have access to some loot-restricted content, or are just there to fill in an armor class requirement. In some cases, the 3rd and 4th choices are of equal relevance.

The Kyrian

  • Anima Conductor
    • Tier 1: https://www.wowhead.com/object=356818/penitence-of-purity contains https://www.wowhead.com/item=184296/puritys-edge-greatblade, only lootable by Plate classes.
    • Tier 3: https://www.wowhead.com/npc=156340/larionrider-orstus drops https://www.wowhead.com/item=184365/aegis-of-salvation only for Paladins, Warriors and Shamans.
  • Path of Ascension (Special Building)
    • Thankfully, once you build Tier 5, you can purchase all building-related rewards and learn them on any character, except the Ensembles, which you can mail to other Kyrian alts of the proper armor type.
    • In addition, this is the only special Covenant building where you can purchase the shared "special Covenant building" rewards for Anima, while it's quite RNG-based for the other Covenants.
  • In Korthia, the https://www.wowhead.com/npc=180032/wild-worldcracker rare event can only be started by a Kyrian and drops  https://www.wowhead.com/item=187380/devourer-hide-belt. 

There are no loot restricted rewards associated with the Kyrians' Transport Network.

The Necrolords

  • Anima Conductor
    • Tier 1: https://www.wowhead.com/object=355035/chosen-runecoffer drops https://www.wowhead.com/item=183622/grand-runespeakers-staff for Cloth classes, Evokers, Monks, Shamans and Druids.
    • Tier 1: https://www.wowhead.com/npc=162741/gieger drops https://www.wowhead.com/item=184298/amalgamated-forsworns-journal for all of the above classes, as well as Paladins and Demon Hunters.
    • Tier 3: https://www.wowhead.com/npc=168147/sabriel-the-bonecleaver drops https://www.wowhead.com/item=184291/tempered-boneplate-waistguard.
  • Abomination Factory (Special Building)
    • This one is the big nightmare folks.
    • Each NPC with a weekly quest has one or more cosmetic items that can randomly show up as a quest reward. Most of them award a cosmetic with an armor type for each class.
    • When reaching Tier 5 you unlock the ability to get Unity set piece cosmetic rewards from some World Quests.
      • You must have the Unity companion with you, active, while doing the quest.
      • This reward is hidden until the World Quest is complete.
      • The cosmetic will be one you haven't learned.
      • There is a Unity set for each armor type.
      • I highly suggest finding Discord servers where folks announce when they happen upon a World Quest with this hidden reward.
    • Basically, you need one character of each armor type to get a total of 2 cosmetic sets each:
      • One from weekly NPC quests, with more NPCs unlocking as you upgrade this building.
      • One from the Unity system, which unlocks at Tier 5. Yes, you have to grind the Anima and Souls to max this building 4 times.
  • The https://www.wowhead.com/object=352086/blackhound-cache event that starts with donating cloth to https://www.wowhead.com/npc=157843/visectus contains https://www.wowhead.com/item=183619/everlasting-boneforged-greataxe, only lootable by Plate classes.
  • In Korthia, the https://www.wowhead.com/npc=180042/fleshwing rare event can only be started by a Necrolord and drops https://www.wowhead.com/item=187372/miasma-filtering-headpiece.

Night Fae

  • Anima Conductor
    • Tier 1: The Star Lake Amphitheater spawns a random rare once per day. There's no deterministic way to get the one you need. Two of them drop loot-restricted transmog:
      • https://www.wowhead.com/npc=166135/astra-as-azshara drops https://www.wowhead.com/item=179518/glimmerlight-staff (Cloth classes, Evokers, Monks, Shamans, Druids)
      • https://www.wowhead.com/npc=166138/mikai-as-argus-the-unmaker drops https://www.wowhead.com/item=179534/mikais-deathscythe (Druids, Hunters, Monks)
    • Tier 3: https://www.wowhead.com/npc=168647/valfir-the-unrelenting drops https://www.wowhead.com/item=180154/greataxe-of-unrelenting-pursuit (Plate classes)
  • In Korthia, the https://www.wowhead.com/npc=180013/escaped-wilderling rare event can only be started by a Night Fae and drops https://www.wowhead.com/item=187395/reinforced-stygian-spaulders.

The Transport Network and Queen's Conservatory (Special Building) do not have any loot-restricted drops, though it is worth mentioning there's some exclusive random drops from Conservatory content (like https://www.wowhead.com/item=187705/choofas-call) that are worth targeting early, relying on the Kyrian special building to grab most of the shared loot.

The Ember Court & The Avowed

There's no better way to farm reputation and cosmetics from The Avowed faction than by using a Venthyr. By inviting Cryptkeeper Kassir to your party, you can farm a boatload of stuff from https://www.wowhead.com/object=356752/cryptkeeper-kassirs-extravagant-tribute :

  • Sinstones of any quality for summoning rares.
  • https://www.wowhead.com/item=172957/atonement-crypt-key 
  • https://www.wowhead.com/item=172965/sinstone-fragments (needs citation)

In fact, if you're looking to target farm a specific Inquisitor and their sinstone, do the following:

  • Keep having parties with Cryptkeeper Kassir.
  • Loot his tribute, getting a random sinstone each time.
    • You'll always get a sinstone you do not currently have.
  • When you have all but one sinstone, you can guarantee you'll always get that particular sinstone in his tribute.

Luckily, he's also a Tier 1 guest, meaning you can run this on several characters with little upfront cost. This is extremely important as Inquistors have a ridiculous amount of low-drop cosmetics. Aside from way too many cloaks to list here, they may also drop:

Other Considerations

  • Technically, each class has a Legendary Runecarver memory item unique to each Covenant. While very bothersome, you can just run a character through each Covenant real quick to get those and learn them in Torghast, with no further commitment.
  • As much as possible, try to have your main Covenant characters be Engineers. Travelling through the Shadowlands is a nightmare without a wormhole.
  • Nathria weapon tokens like https://www.wowhead.com/item=183893/abominable-anima-spherule require a complex setup of Class x Covenant to be used efficiently. Having at least a character of each class spread around all the Covenants is recommended.
