World Quest Weapons

You find the rare world quest on Zandalar or Kul Tiras that awards a weapon. You've been hunting those appearances for weeks or months. You go to WoWhead, check the world quest, and see the full list of weapons available as rewards.

You see you already own the one-handed sword being displayed as a reward on your current character, but not the two-handed sword that can also be earned. You switch from Protection to Fury and wait a minute, but nothing happens. Unlike in Zereth Mortis or other zones, the reward doesn't change.

This is because those BFA world quests function differently. The reward is determined by your class rather than your spec.

We'll use the world quest above as an example. See the list of rewards? This means that each of these rewards is available to at least one class. You can do the following to ensure you get everything you're missing before the world quest expires:

  1. Check to see which of these weapon appearances you're missing.
    1. You can find these in /att -> Outdoor Zones -> Zandalar / Kul Tiras -> Zone Rewards
  2. Open the page for each item you're missing.
  3. Check the loot eligibily under the item tooltip. One of these classes will have it as a reward.
  4. Log on each of the listed classes until you find the one that has the reward. Repeat for every item on the list.

Ideally, you would have 1 character of each class with their BFA intro completed, and world quests unlocked. Note that Evokers, though released post-BFA, also get one of these as a reward, and can be particularly useful for hunting down Intellect-based two-handed swords.
